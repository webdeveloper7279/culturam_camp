ABOUT PROGRAM

.

Applications are now open for the Uzbekistan Cultural Camp 2025, a unique cultural immersion program that brings together young people from around the world! This 6-day cultural exchange program will take place across the historic cities of Shahrisabz, Samarkand, and Bukhara — the heart of the Silk Road.
The Uzbekistan Cultural Camp is open to applicants from all nationalities and any academic background. No language tests like IELTS are required. The program is designed to deepen participants’ understanding of Uzbek culture, history, and traditions, while also offering the chance to connect with international peers through shared experiences.
Throughout the camp, participants will explore ancient monuments, join traditional craft workshops, learn to cook authentic Uzbek dishes, and take part in local festivals and dances.
The program includes cultural activities, excursions, and hands-on learning. Scroll down to learn more about the program details, eligibility requirements, and what you'll gain from this unforgettable experience in Uzbekistan!

CULTURAL CAMP

Cultural Camp in Uzbekistan project's aim is to promote cultural exchange and understanding among participants from different backgrounds. The camp seeks to provide an immersive experience in Uzbek culture, traditions, and history while fostering dialogue and collaboration among attendees. Through various activities such as workshops, performances, and discussions, the project aims to enhance participants' appreciation of Uzbekistan's rich heritage and encourage them to share their own cultural experiences. Ultimately, the camp aspires to build lasting connections and friendships that transcend borders.
