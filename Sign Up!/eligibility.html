ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

.
The Cultural Camp in Uzbekistan welcomes passionate young individuals from all nationalities aged 16 to 26. Ideal candidates are those with a strong interest in travel, the Uzbek language, and culture, and who actively engage in social or youth initiatives. Applicants should demonstrate leadership qualities, possess excellent English communication skills, and be physically and mentally fit to travel during the program dates.

1.The Program is open to all the Nationalities
2.Applicants Age: 16 – 26 years old
3.Must have a Strong interest in Travel, Uzbek language and Culture
4.Active involvement in social and youth activities
5.Demonstrated leadership qualities
6.Excellent English communication skills
7.Ability to travel to Uzbekistan during the program dates
8.Must be in Good physical and mental health

CULTURAL CAMP

Cultural Camp in Uzbekistan project's aim is to promote cultural exchange and understanding among participants from different backgrounds. The camp seeks to provide an immersive experience in Uzbek culture, traditions, and history while fostering dialogue and collaboration among attendees. Through various activities such as workshops, performances, and discussions, the project aims to enhance participants' appreciation of Uzbekistan's rich heritage and encourage them to share their own cultural experiences. Ultimately, the camp aspires to build lasting connections and friendships that transcend borders.
