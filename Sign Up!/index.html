CULTURAL CAMP

Cultural Camp in Uzbekistan project's aim is to promote cultural exchange and understanding among participants from different backgrounds. The camp seeks to provide an immersive experience in Uzbek culture, traditions, and history while fostering dialogue and collaboration among attendees. Through various activities such as workshops, performances, and discussions, the project aims to enhance participants' appreciation of Uzbekistan's rich heritage and encourage them to share their own cultural experiences. Ultimately, the camp aspires to build lasting connections and friendships that transcend borders.
