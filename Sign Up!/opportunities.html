Participants will spend six days immersing themselves in the rich history, traditions, and customs of Uzbekistan. Throughout the program, they will visit historic cities such as Shahrisabz, Samarkand, and Bukhara, exploring ancient monuments and learning about their historical significance.

The program includes the following cultural activities:
1.Explore three historic cities: Shahrisabz, Samarkand, and Bukhara
2.Deepen understanding of Uzbek culture through food, lifestyle, and traditional crafts
3.Cultural Tour Activities at the Crossroads of the Silk Road
4.Explore Uzbek cuisine, including plov, manti, shashlik, and other regional specialties
5.Participate in hands-on activities such as pottery making and traditional dance workshops
6.Engage in a Heritage Walking Tour of Samarkand

CULTURAL CAMP

Cultural Camp in Uzbekistan project's aim is to promote cultural exchange and understanding among participants from different backgrounds. The camp seeks to provide an immersive experience in Uzbek culture, traditions, and history while fostering dialogue and collaboration among attendees. Through various activities such as workshops, performances, and discussions, the project aims to enhance participants' appreciation of Uzbekistan's rich heritage and encourage them to share their own cultural experiences. Ultimately, the camp aspires to build lasting connections and friendships that transcend borders.
